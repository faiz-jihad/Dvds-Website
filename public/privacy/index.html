Google Sign-In & OAuth 2.0 User Data Disclosures

1. Google OAuth 2.0 & Google User Data Policy

DVDs Zone offers customers the option to create an account and authenticate using Google Sign-In (Google OAuth 2.0). When you choose to sign in or register with your Google Account, we access and process specific information from your Google account strictly as outlined below:

A. What Google Data We Access:

  • Your Name: Used to personalize your customer profile and generate shipping labels for physical DVD deliveries.
  • Your Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier to send transactional order confirmations, dispatch receipts, Royal Mail tracking numbers, and customer support replies.
  • Your Profile Picture URL: Used to display your customer avatar in the navigation bar and account profile dashboard.
  • Google User ID (sub identifier): A unique technical string provided by Google to securely authenticate and link your account sessions.

Note: We only request standard basic profile and email scopes (openid, profile, email). We do NOT request or access your Google contacts, Google Drive files, calendar, location history, or any other sensitive or restricted Google user data.

B. How We Use Google User Data:

  • To securely authenticate and log you into your DVDs Zone customer account without requiring a separate password.
  • To associate your physical DVD box set orders, delivery addresses, and saved wishlist items with your profile.
  • To provide customer support and service regarding your physical orders.

C. Strict Non-Sale & Protection of Google User Data:

  • No Sale of Data: DVDs Zone never sells, rents, leases, or trades Google user data to any third party, data broker, or marketing entity.
  • No Advertising Use: We do NOT share your Google user data with advertising platforms or track you across third-party websites.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance Statement: DVDs Zone's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

D. Google User Data Retention & Permanent Deletion:

  • Retention: Your Google profile information is retained only as long as you maintain an active customer account with DVDs Zone.
  • User-Requested Deletion: You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your account and all associated Google user data at any time. Simply send an email to azrayanltd@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account & Google Data". All account profile records and authentication tokens will be permanently and irreversibly purged within 30 days.
  • Revoking Access: You can also immediately revoke DVDs Zone's permission to access your Google account at any time directly through your Google Security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

2. Physical Order Fulfilment & Payment Processing

When purchasing physical DVD box sets, films, or merchandise from DVDs Zone, we collect necessary transactional information:

  • Delivery Information: Full recipient name, physical delivery address, and contact phone number. This information is shared strictly with postal carriers (Royal Mail) solely to fulfill shipping and track physical deliveries across the UK and internationally.
  • Payment Information: All card payments and digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) are processed directly by PCI-DSS certified payment processors (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. and PayPal Holdings, Inc.). DVDs Zone never views, handles, or stores your raw credit or debit card numbers, CVC codes, or banking credentials.
  • Statutory Invoicing: Basic order receipts are retained for 6 years in accordance with UK statutory HMRC accounting laws.

3. 100% Public Access • No Login Required

Browsing and shopping on DVDs Zone is completely open to the public. You do NOT need to create an account or sign in to browse our complete catalogue, view item availability, or complete a purchase. Guest checkout is fully supported for all customers.

4. Data Security & Infrastructure

We implement robust technical and operational security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: Enforced via HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Database Isolation: User profiles are hosted on hardened database infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Storage at Rest: Database backups and system configurations are protected with AES-256 encryption.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

DVDs Zone uses strictly necessary cookies and browser local storage solely to provide core website functionality:

  • Maintaining items in your shopping basket as you browse catalogue categories.
  • Storing your secure authentication session when you log in.
  • Remembering your visual interface preference (light or dark mode).

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

6. Your Statutory Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, you possess comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the permanent deletion of your customer account and personal data.
  • Right to Restriction: Request the restriction of processing your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your personal information in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by telephone on 0303 123 1113.
